**Authors:** Pedro Ribeiro, Martim Afonso, André Vasconcelos, João F. Ferreira

We introduce VerusWeave, the first benchmark dedicated to concurrent proof repair. It comprises 75 focused tasks and 17 full-module variants drawn from four open-source projects. Each task removes proof annotations that an agent must reconstruct, while the executable code and specifications remain intact; repairs are checked with Verus together with a change checker. In an initial evaluation of four general-purpose coding-agent harnesses, success rates range from 75–85% on focused tasks but drop to 29–47% on full-module variants, indicating that autonomous module-wide proof repair for concurrent programs remains a challenging open problem.
