**Authors:** Martim Afonso, Praphulla M. S. Bhawsar, Monjoy Saha, Jonas S. Almeida, Arlindo L. Oliveira

Whole Slide Images (WSIs) are high-resolution digital scans of microscope slides for which diagnostic and genetic labels are typically recorded at the slide level rather than for individual tiles. We apply weakly supervised Multiple Instance Learning (MIL) to two cancer types from The Cancer Genome Atlas, invasive breast carcinoma (TCGA-BRCA) and lung squamous cell carcinoma (TCGA-LUSC), evaluating tumor detection at low magnification and TP53 mutation detection across several magnifications. We compare Attention MIL (AMIL) and a new Additive MIL (AdMIL) implementation: the additive method matches a reference implementation (AUC 0.96) while Attention MIL performs slightly better (AUC 0.97). The architectures respond to different morphological features at different magnification levels, and TP53 mutation prediction relies most on higher magnifications where cellular structure is most visible. We further use the models' activation scores to produce interpretability heatmaps that highlight regions of interest.
