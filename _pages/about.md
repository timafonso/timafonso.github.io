I'm a **PhD candidate in Computer Science** at the University of Lisbon, affiliated with [INESC-ID](https://www.inesc-id.pt/) and [Instituto Superior Técnico](https://tecnico.ulisboa.pt/). My research focuses on **automating the formal verification of software systems**.

My current work centers on **Agentic Theorem Proving** — using language models and machine learning to drive interactive theorem provers and verification tools (such as [Lean](https://leanprover.github.io/) and [Verus](https://github.com/verus-lang/verus)), so that correctness proofs about software can be found with far less manual effort. I'm also interested in **explainable AI**, in particular bringing it to agent-assisted formal verification so that the proofs these agents produce — and the decisions they make along the way — are transparent and trustworthy.

## Research interests

- Automated Theorem Proving
- Formal verification of software
- Machine Learning and Language Models
- Explainable AI

## Selected work

- **ProfOlaf** — a semi-automated tool for systematic literature reviews, combining snowballing with LLM-assisted analysis (FSE 2026).
- **ProofCoder** — an agentic proof-synthesis system for the Rocq proof assistant that reasons continuously throughout proof search ([preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14106)).
- **SeVerus** — context-aware, gamified benchmarking for Rust (Verus) verification, built from the real proof dependencies of verified projects ([preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07194)).
