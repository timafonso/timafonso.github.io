I'm a **PhD candidate in Computer Science** at the University of Lisbon, affiliated with [INESC-ID](https://www.inesc-id.pt/) and [Instituto Superior Técnico](https://tecnico.ulisboa.pt/). My research focuses on **automating the formal verification of software systems**.

My current work centers on **Agentic Theorem Proving** — using language models and machine learning to drive interactive theorem provers and verification tools (such as [Lean](https://leanprover.github.io/) and [Verus](https://github.com/verus-lang/verus)), so that correctness proofs about software can be found with far less manual effort. I'm also interested in explainable AI and learning-based fault localization, which I worked on as a visiting student at Carnegie Mellon University.

## Research interests

- Automated Theorem Proving and the Lean theorem prover
- Formal verification of software
- Machine Learning and Language Models
- Explainable AI

## Selected work

- **VerusWeave** — the first benchmark for proof repair in concurrent Verus programs ([preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00287)).
- **ProfOlaf** — a semi-automated tool for systematic literature reviews, combining snowballing with LLM-assisted analysis (FSE 2026).
- **Multiple Instance Learning for WSI** — a comparison of attention-based MIL methods for cancer detection in whole slide images (*Journal of Pathology Informatics*, 2024).
