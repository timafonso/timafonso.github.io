**ProfOlaf** is a semi-automated tool that streamlines systematic reviews and mapping studies while preserving methodological rigor.

It supports iterative **snowballing** to collect candidate articles from an initial seed, with **human-in-the-loop** filtering during screening, and uses **large language models** to assist with selecting articles, extracting key topics, and answering questions about article content. ProfOlaf is available both as a command-line tool and as a web application.

- **Code:** [github.com/sr-lab/ProfOlaf](https://github.com/sr-lab/ProfOlaf)
- **Paper:** *ProfOlaf: Semi-Automated Tool for Systematic Literature Reviews* (FSE 2026) — [arXiv:2510.26750](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26750)
